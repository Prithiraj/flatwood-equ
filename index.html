Before you saddle up

A few useful answers.

Do you offer both English and Western riding?

Yes. Multiple public equestrian directories list Flatwoods as offering both English and Western lessons. Call to confirm the current lesson schedule and horse availability.

Can I book a trail ride?

Trail riding and horseback riding are publicly listed services. Because current ride formats, eligibility, and pricing are not published consistently, call or text the barn before making plans.

Do you board horses?

Boarding is publicly listed, along with an on-site trainer. Contact Flatwoods directly for current space, care options, requirements, and monthly pricing.

What should a first-time visitor know?

The most important step is to call first. Ask about rider age/experience fit, clothing and helmet requirements, arrival time, weather policy, pricing, and any waiver requirements.
